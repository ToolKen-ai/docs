---
title: "BYOK — Bring Your Own Key"
description: "Your provider API keys are forwarded untouched — Toolken never stores them."
---

Toolken operates on a strict BYOK model: your provider credentials (`Authorization: Bearer …` for OpenAI-compatible providers, `x-api-key: …` for Anthropic) are passed through to the upstream provider verbatim and are never logged or stored by Toolken. The only credential Toolken reads is your `X-Toolken-Key`, which is stripped before the request leaves the gateway.
