title: "Your First Cost Report"
description: "Send a few requests and see your spend broken down by feature and customer in the Toolken dashboard."
---

After routing requests through the gateway with attribution headers set, open the Toolken dashboard and navigate to the Analytics page. Within seconds of your first proxied call you will see cost aggregated by model, feature, and customer — no configuration required beyond the `X-Toolken-Key` header and the optional metadata headers described in the Attribution section.
