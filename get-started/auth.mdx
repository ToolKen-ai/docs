---
title: "Authentication"
description: "How Toolken authenticates your requests and forwards your provider credentials."
---

Every request to the Toolken gateway must carry an `X-Toolken-Key` header — this is Toolken's own authentication token, issued from your dashboard. Your upstream provider credentials (e.g. `Authorization: Bearer sk-…` for OpenAI-compatible providers, or `x-api-key: sk-ant-…` for Anthropic) are forwarded verbatim to the provider and are never stored by Toolken — this is the BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model.
