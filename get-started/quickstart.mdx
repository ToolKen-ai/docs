---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Route your first LLM request through Toolken in under two minutes."
---

Point your OpenAI-compatible client at `https://gateway.toolken.ai` and add your `X-Toolken-Key` header — that's the entire integration. Your existing provider credentials (`Authorization: Bearer …` or `x-api-key: …`) pass through untouched to the upstream provider.

```bash
curl https://gateway.toolken.ai/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Toolken-Key: tk_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"gpt-4o-mini","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
```
