---
title: "What is Toolken?"
description: "The zero-infra edge gateway that tracks LLM costs per agent, feature, and customer — and flags where to cut."
---

Toolken is an edge gateway that sits between your application and any LLM provider, capturing every token spent and attributing it to the right agent, feature, or customer in real time — no SDK changes, no data pipelines, no infrastructure to manage. You get a live spend intelligence layer: cost dashboards, budget guardrails, and optimization signals, all from a single base-URL swap.
