---
title: "Rate Limits"
description: "Cap requests per second or per minute to protect against traffic spikes."
---

Rate limits in Toolken are enforced via atomic Redis counters at the edge, before any provider call is made. When the limit is reached the gateway returns `429 rate_limited`. Limits can be configured globally per tenant or scoped to individual features or customers, giving you fine-grained control over who can make how many calls in a given window.
