---
title: "Budgets"
description: "Set monthly, daily, or per-customer spend caps that the gateway enforces in real time."
---

Budgets are configured per-tenant in the Toolken dashboard and checked atomically on every request. When cumulative spend reaches the cap, the gateway immediately returns `402 budget_exceeded` — no further requests are forwarded to the provider until the budget resets or is raised. Budget windows, thresholds, and alert rules are all managed without code changes.
