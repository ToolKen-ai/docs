---
title: "Pricing Calculation"
description: "How Toolken calculates cost from token counts and live provider pricing data."
---

Toolken calculates cost per request from the token counts returned by the provider and live pricing data maintained in the Toolken pricing database (the open-source `@toolken/models` package). Costs are stored with full precision and surfaced in the dashboard as USD. Pricing data is kept current as providers update their rates — no configuration required on your end.
